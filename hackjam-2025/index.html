Hackjam 2025: My First Hackathon (That We Also Won)#

During Fall 2025, I participated in my first ever hackathon, Hackjam 2025. In a team of three with Robert French and Nguyen Phuc Tran. Hackjam is a beginner-friendly, less than 24 hour hackathon hosted by the Society of Hispanic Professionals and Engineers (SHPE). Going into it, I had no idea what to expect, but I was certainly surprised.

Ultimately, we created a login portal for a Scottish travel website, which played lovely music if you did not login fast enough. Check it out here!

Planning the Project#

There were three tracks that were available in Hackjam 2025:

  • Track #1. Tech for Good / USF: Create a Tech Solution that improves life at USD
  • Track #2. AI Innovation / Everyday AI: Build a creative AI-powered solution that makes everyday life simpler, smarter, or more engaging.
  • Track #3. Worst UI Design: Take a standard login screen and turn it into the most annoying, funny, and over-complicated experience possible.

You might have heard of the joke before, "Graphics design is my passion," when the reality is that you're terrible at designing graphics. I knew that I could channel that idea into creating the worst UI design for a login page. I recently had heard the "Scotland Forever" audio, which literally says "Scotland Forever" repeatedly with very loud bagpipes playing in the background. This was our basis for the login page.

Going into Motion#

Once I got the idea, Robert and Tran worked on coding a simple HTMl and Javascript website (with some help of Github Copilot). During that time, I worked on editing the audio to make it even more of an annoyance once it plays.

The Final Product#

After making the login page very annoying, we set about a 20-30 second timer once you enter the login page before the audio goes on, so as to make it a challenge to avoid the "Scotland Forever" audio. Furthermore, we themed the login page to be a Scottish travel portal, sort of like a travel agency but for specifically Scotland.

Once we submitted the project in the afternoon, Robert and I both left to do more homework, while Tran stayed in the area to also do homework. Later that evening, I received a message that we actually won! This was very surprised, as I didn't expect my joke of an idea would actually help us win the track. Nonetheless, it shows to me that all ideas need to be considered, and to not count something out just because it seems ridiculous.
